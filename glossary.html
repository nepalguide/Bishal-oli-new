Semantic SEO & Entity Glossary

Master the core concepts of Semantic SEO, Knowledge Graphs, vector embeddings, and Generative Engine Optimization (GEO).

Entity (in Search)

A unique, well-defined concept or object (such as a person, place, organization, or methodology) that is distinguishable from other concepts. Google uses entities to build Knowledge Graphs.

Topical Map

An architectural structure that defines all required sub-topics, entity relationships, and node hierarchies needed for a domain to achieve complete authority in its industry.

Generative Engine Optimization (GEO)

The practice of structuring content, schema graphs, and fact-dense statements to earn citations inside AI Overviews and conversational LLMs (ChatGPT, Perplexity, Gemini).

Vector Embedding

A numerical representation of text in a high-dimensional vector space, allowing search engines to calculate contextual semantic similarity between queries and web pages.
