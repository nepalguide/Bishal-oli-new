OFFICIAL GOOGLE ALGORITHM FACTORS

The 3 Pillars of Local Search Ranking

PILLAR 01

Relevance

How well a local Business Profile matches what someone is searching for. Complete, detailed business information helps Google understand your business entity and match your profile to relevant searches.

PILLAR 02

Distance

How far each potential search result is from the location term used in a search or the user's geolocated position. If a user doesn't specify a location, Google calculates distance based on known location data.

PILLAR 03

Prominence

How well-known or established a business is. Prominence is based on information Google gathers about a business from across the web, including links, articles, directories, review counts, and overall organic website authority.
