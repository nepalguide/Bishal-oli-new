BIOGRAPHY & BACKGROUND

Who Is Bishal Oli?

I am Bishal Oli, Founder & CEO at GrowZari and a leading search engine optimization consultant based in Tarakeshwar, Kathmandu, Nepal. My work focuses on bridging the gap between mathematical search engine architecture (information retrieval, entity disambiguation, and vector similarity) and real-world commercial growth for businesses.

Rather than viewing search engine optimization as a collection of isolated tricks or keyword-stuffing tactics, I treat search engines as structured database systems. I design comprehensive Topical Authority Maps, optimize entity relationships in JSON-LD schema, and build scalable search architectures that earn sustainable rankings in both traditional Google search and modern AI Overviews (ChatGPT, Perplexity, Gemini).

My clients range from local Nepalese businesses (trekking agencies, high-value service providers, and local brands) to international SaaS platforms and e-commerce companies seeking global organic lead pipelines.
